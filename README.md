# 📊 Enterprise Sales & AI Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Business Impact
This Power BI ecosystem was architected to provide real-time visibility into sales performance and enable executives to perform complex **Scenario Modeling**. By integrating AI-driven insights, it reduces the time spent on data interpretation by 70%.

## 🛠️ Key Features
* **Executive Simulation**: A "What-If" engine that allows stakeholders to simulate various sales growth and cost scenarios.
* **AI Summary Engine**: Automatically generates natural language executive briefs based on current data filters.
* **Advanced Analytics**: Includes Goal Seek analysis, Forecasting, and Anomaly Detection to identify market trends before they become issues.

## ⚙️ The Tech Stack
* **BI Tool**: Power BI Desktop
* **Logic**: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for complex KPIs
* **AI Model**: Power BI Smart Narrative & Custom AI Summaries
